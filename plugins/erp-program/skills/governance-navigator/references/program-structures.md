# ERP FORWARD — Program Structures (authoritative reference)

Source: "ERP Implementation Organizational Structure" and "ERP Forward Governance Structure" charts, transcribed 2026-08. Both charts note the structure reflects current expectations and is subject to change — changes to this file come through pull request, and this file is the single source all governance answers cite.

## 1. The decision escalation ladder (governance pyramid)

Decisions are made at the lowest tier that has the authority, and escalate upward only when they cannot be resolved. The tiers, bottom to top:

| Tier | Who | Role in decisions |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Project Teams | Do the work; resolve day-to-day questions within their team's scope |
| Tier 2 | Workstream Leads | Decide within their workstream; first stop when a project team is stuck or a question crosses teams inside one workstream |
| Tier 3 | Program Management Team (PMO + all Workstream Leads) | Decide cross-workstream questions: scope, sequencing, resources, standards |
| Tier 4 | Steering Committee | Decide program-level questions: budget, timeline, major scope, policy conflicts, risk acceptance |
| Tier 5 | Executive Committee | Final authority: institutional commitments, go/no-go, matters affecting university strategy |

Input channels (these ADVISE — they are not part of the command chain):
- Advisory Committees provide input and feedback to Tiers 3–4 (Steering Committee and Program Management Team level).
- System, University, and MU Health constituents and the Functional SME Committees provide input and feedback to Tiers 1–2.

## 2. Governance and advisory bodies (org chart, top section)

Decision-making governance committees:
- Executive Committee (top)
- Steering Committee (reports to Executive Committee)
- High-Level Advisory Committee (alongside Steering — advisory)

Other existing advisory bodies (input, not command): Council of Chancellors (COC), UM Academic Officers (UMAO), Chief Information Officers (CIOs).

SME Committees (part-time functional expertise and support — they advise, they do not approve):
- Finance side: Admin Mgmt Council (AMC) · Accounting / Record-to-Report Committee · Research/Grants Committee · Procure-to-Pay Committee
- HR side: HR Council (HRC) · Core HR Committee · Pay & Benefits Committee · Recruitment & Talent Management Committee

## 3. Project Management Office (PMO)

Functions inside the PMO: Project Management · Testing · Cutover · Student System Planning.
The PMO sits between the governance committees and the five workstreams, and together with the Workstream Leads forms the Tier-3 Program Management Team.

## 4. The five workstreams (org chart, bottom section)

Every project team belongs to exactly one workstream; some workstreams have a pillar-lead level between the workstream lead and the teams.

**Change Management (Change Mgmt Lead)** — teams: Training · Campus Engagement · Communications. No pillar level.

**Finance (FIN Lead)** — three pillars:
- Accounting / Record-to-Report Lead → teams: GL/Financial Accounting · Planning/Budgets · Business Assets · Payroll Accounting · Receivables · Projects (Non-Sponsored) · Banking/Cash Mgmt · Endowments
- Research/Grants Lead → team: Research/Grants
- Procure-to-Pay Lead → teams: Purchasing/Sourcing · Inventory · Supplier Acct/Accounts Payable · Expenses

**HR (HR Lead)** — three pillars:
- Core HR Lead → teams: Core HR · Compensation
- Pay & Benefits Lead → teams: Payroll · Time & Absence · Benefits
- Recruitment & Talent Management Lead → teams: Recruiting & Onboarding · Learning · Talent & Performance

**Shared Capabilities (Shared Capabilities Lead)** — teams: Reporting · Application Security. No pillar level.

**Technical (Technical Lead)** — teams: ERP Integrations · HR/Finance Technical Team · Security. No pillar level.

## 5. Chain-of-command rules derived from the two charts

1. A project team member's first stop is their own team, then their pillar lead (where one exists: FIN and HR), then their workstream lead.
2. A workstream lead who cannot resolve an issue, or whose issue touches another workstream, brings it to the Program Management Team (Tier 3).
3. The PMO escalates Tier-3-unresolvable items to the Steering Committee; Steering escalates institution-level matters to the Executive Committee.
4. SME Committees and advisory bodies are consulted for input at any point — but consultation is not approval, and their feedback does not substitute for a decision by the accountable tier.
5. Payroll questions have two homes by topic: Payroll operations/design → HR workstream, Pay & Benefits pillar. Payroll accounting/GL → Finance workstream, Accounting/Record-to-Report pillar. Cross-cutting payroll issues (most of the hard ones) go to Tier 3.
6. Testing, cutover, and student-system planning questions go to the PMO's own functions, not to a workstream.
7. Reporting and application security → Shared Capabilities. Integrations and technical infrastructure → Technical. Security has two homes: application/role security → Shared Capabilities; technical/platform security → Technical. When unsure, ask via your workstream lead to Tier 3.
